## 🌟 What is BrowseCurator?

The internet is full of wonders, learning tools, and creativity for curious minds—but it can also unexpectedly expose children to harmful, or illicit material.

**BrowseCurator** is an easy-to-use browser extension designed specifically for parents. Acting as a quiet digital guardian, BrowseCurator helps shield your family from:

- 🚫 **Illicit & Explicit Imagery**: Automatically screening and blocking indecent visual content.
- 🔕 **Vulgar & Harmful Language**: Filtering out profanity.
- ⚠️ **Unsafe Webpages**: Halting access to dangerous or illicit pages before they can load.

---

## 💡 Why BrowseCurator?

Traditional web filters are often complicated, hard to configure, or compromise personal privacy. BrowseCurator is guided by three simple principles:

### 1. 🏡 Designed for Parents, Not Tech Experts
No complicated network configuration or IT degree required. Clear controls, simple sliders, and zero confusing jargon.

### 2. 🔒 Privacy-First & Local
Your family's browsing habits are private. BrowseCurator is being built to analyze content directly on your device wherever possible—no tracking, no data selling, and no invasive surveillance.

### 3. 🌱 Gentle & Reassuring
Rather than displaying harsh warnings or technical error codes, children who reach a blocked page are greeted with a warm, kid-friendly screen explaining that the page isn't age-appropriate. (????)

## 🎯 Planned Features

| Feature | What It Does for Your Family |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🖼️ **Smart Image Shield** | Scans and blurs or blocks mature, explicit, or disturbing images in real time. |
| 💬 **Clean Language Filter** | Catches harsh profanity, slurs, and explicit sexual discussions on web pages. |
| 🛡️ **Instant Page Blocker** | Immediately intervenes if a webpage crosses the safety threshold. |
| 🔑 **Parent Passcode Override** | Quick PIN protection allowing parents to easily unlock trusted pages when needed. |
| 📋 **Custom Family Allow / Block Lists** | Freedom to whitelist family-approved educational sites or block specific domains completely. |
